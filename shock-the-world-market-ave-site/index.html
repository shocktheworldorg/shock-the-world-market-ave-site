The Site

One block. Four parcels. Shovel-ready.

The campus sits on a corner the whole neighborhood passes every day — four adjacent parcels totaling about a third of an acre, already under site control and in active restoration.

  • 0.35 acres across four contiguous parcels
  • 1940s shipyard-worker-era buildings
  • Active site work already underway
The Vision

Restored worker-era property, returned to the community that built it.

For generations, North Richmond has carried the weight of heavy industry without sharing in its prosperity. The Market Ave Community Impact Campus changes that story — not by tearing down, but by restoring.

We are taking buildings that housed wartime shipyard workers and giving them a second life: a place where local residents train for real careers, where young people find a safe foundation, and where clean air and green space are treated as a right, not a luxury.

Left: the structure taken back to its bones · Right: the building today, carrying the North Richmond Pride mural
Adaptive Reuse

We build on what's already standing.

These are 1940s shipyard-worker structures — built fast, built to last, and still standing eighty years later. Rather than demolish that history, we're rehabilitating it: keeping the bones, upgrading the systems, and making every visible improvement count.

Adaptive reuse keeps embodied carbon in place, honors the workers who came before, and delivers a community asset faster and greener than ground-up construction ever could.

The Build Scope

Six upgrades that turn a block into a campus.

A coordinated rehabilitation plan — healthy buildings, clean energy, and usable green space.

01

Building Rehabilitation

Structural repair, envelope, roofing, and finishes that bring the shipyard-era buildings up to a safe, modern standard.

02

Electrical Modernization

Full electrical upgrade with solar-and-battery readiness, preparing the campus for clean, resilient power.

03

Plumbing & Restrooms

New plumbing and public restrooms sized for training cohorts, events, and daily community use.

04

HVAC & Indoor Air Quality

Efficient heating, cooling, and filtration that protects health in a community burdened by industrial air pollution.

05

All-Electric Appliances

Clean, all-electric kitchen and building systems — no on-site combustion, lower emissions, lower bills.

06

Green Space & Native Planting

Outdoor gathering space, native landscaping, and redwood fencing that make the campus a place people want to be.

What Happens Here

Two engines of opportunity, under one roof.

Workforce Development Center

Hands-on training in the trades that are hiring right now — plumbing, HVAC, electrical, construction, and green-energy jobs — delivered locally, to local residents.

  • High-skills classes taught by working professionals
  • Job development and direct placement support
  • A pipeline into the campus's own clean-energy buildout

Adult & Youth Safe Zone

A welcoming space for ages 13–24 — built around safety, mentorship, and a real path forward. Life skills, leadership, and a creative hub where young people are seen and supported.

  • After-school and weekend programming for ages 13–24
  • Mentorship, life skills, and leadership development
  • A youth creative hub and safe community gathering point
